Privacy policy

Effective 28 October 2026, updated 24 November 2026. Applies to the PleatView TV app, the PleatView Home Assistant integration and this website, including its enquiry form, developed and published by Hustlesman.

Summary

The PleatView TV app and Home Assistant integration do not collect, sell or share personal data. They have no accounts, analytics, advertising, crash reporting or tracking. The TV app talks only to your own Home Assistant server, and to the media, image and web addresses your Home Assistant sends it. This website keeps only what you send us through its enquiry form, to answer you.

Who we are

PleatView is developed and published by Hustlesman. For anything about privacy, email privacy@hustlesman.com.

The TV app

On the TV itself, PleatView stores:

  • the address of your Home Assistant server and an access token used to show your dashboards,
  • its settings (dashboards, zoom, kiosk mode and similar),
  • a device identifier derived from Android's per-app identifier, so Home Assistant recognises the TV,
  • one-way hashes of the pairing tokens it has issued.

This data never leaves the TV except to your own Home Assistant. It is excluded from Android backups and device-to-device transfers. PleatView does not collect location, contacts, advertising identifiers, usage statistics or crash reports.

Network connections

The app listens on your local network (port 8765) so a paired Home Assistant can control it, and announces itself on the local network so Home Assistant can find it. Pairing requires a PIN shown on the TV. The app connects to your Home Assistant server and to the media, image and web addresses Home Assistant asks it to show. It makes no other connections.

The Home Assistant integration

The integration runs inside your own Home Assistant. By default it creates a local-only Home Assistant user for the TV and gives the TV an access token for that user. Removing the integration deletes that user and resets the TV.

This website

pleatview.hustlesman.com uses no analytics or trackers, and sets no cookies of its own. Its demo wall page shows the YouTube videos named in its address, in YouTube's privacy-enhanced player; YouTube's privacy policy covers those players. The site is hosted on GitHub Pages and delivered through Cloudflare. Like any website host, they process technical data such as IP addresses to deliver pages and protect against abuse, under their own privacy policies. Cloudflare's protection against bots sets one cookie, cf_clearance, which remembers the result of its check on your browser for up to a year. The form on the Console page is covered under Enquiries.

Enquiries

The form on the Console page sends us what you type: what you are asking about, your name, work email, organisation, roughly how many screens you have, and your message. We use it only to answer you, and, if you ask for early access, to tell you when the Console opens. We do not share it, sell it or use it for marketing.

Your browser sends it to a small checking service of ours, which passes it on to our own server. There it is kept encrypted, and only the people who answer enquiries can read it. To keep out spam, the checking service counts the messages from each network address for up to a day, in memory only. Your network address is not kept with your enquiry. Cloudflare delivers the form's requests, as it delivers this site's pages.

We keep an enquiry for up to a year after it arrives, then it is deleted. Backups of our server keep a copy only until they are replaced. To see yours or have it deleted sooner, email privacy@hustlesman.com.

Donations

Donations are optional and are processed by Stripe. We receive what Stripe shares with us: your email address, the amount, and your name or message if you give them. We use this only to send receipts, reply to you and keep the records the law requires. We never see your full card details. Stripe's own privacy policy covers the payment itself.

Deleting your data

  • TV app: uninstall it, or clear its data in Android's app settings.
  • Integration: remove it in Home Assistant, which also deletes the user it created.
  • Enquiries: email privacy@hustlesman.com and we'll delete yours. Otherwise each is deleted a year after it arrives.
  • Donation records: email privacy@hustlesman.com and we'll delete anything we're not required to keep.

Security

Control of the TV stays on your local network, pairing needs a PIN shown on the TV, and only hashes of pairing tokens are stored. Please report security problems privately to privacy@hustlesman.com.

Children

PleatView is not directed at children under 13. The TV app and integration collect no personal data from anyone, and the enquiry form is meant for organisations.

App stores

If you install PleatView from an app store, the store's own privacy policy covers the data it collects, such as download and crash statistics.

Changes

Changes to this policy are published on this page and in the project's GitHub repository.
